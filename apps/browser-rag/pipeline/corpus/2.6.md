# Optyma Cooler · p. 6 · entry 2.6

Symptom: Room temperature too high. Possible cause and actions: Too much air may enter through open doors. Check the door seals and replace them if needed. Keep doors shut for as much of the time as possible.
Source: Danfoss Optyma Cooler, page 6, section 13, entry 2.6. Teaching paraphrase.
