# Optyma Cooler · p. 6 · entry 4.4

Symptom: Coil still frosted after defrost. Possible cause and actions: The defrost cycle may be too short. Increase its duration using the defrost thermostat or timer.
Source: Danfoss Optyma Cooler, page 6, section 13, entry 4.4. Teaching paraphrase.
