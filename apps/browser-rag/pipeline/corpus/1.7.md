# Optyma Cooler · p. 6 · entry 1.7

Symptom: Fan not working. Possible cause and actions: Ice or debris may obstruct the fan. The manual lists removing obstructions and checking the fan junction box for a short circuit.
Source: Danfoss Optyma Cooler, page 6, section 13, entry 1.7. Teaching paraphrase.
